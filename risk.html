Risk Management Tools 🛡️

Calculate your risk before every trade. Never risk more than you can afford to lose.

📏

The 1% Rule

Never risk more than 1% of your total account on a single trade. This keeps you in the game even after a losing streak.

🎯

Risk/Reward Ratio

Always aim for at least a 1:2 risk/reward ratio. For every $1 you risk, you should have the potential to make $2.

⛔

Stop Loss

Always set a stop loss before entering a trade. Know your max loss BEFORE you click buy or sell.
